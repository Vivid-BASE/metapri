ABOUT US

The era of Japanese girls’ heavy metal is here.
From topping charts across the US and Europe to selling out world tours, Japanese female metal acts have become a true global force. Now, a powerful new project emerges at the forefront of this movement.

Heavy Metal Princess Academy

An elite training collective featuring legendary members from Aldious, Mary's Blood, and CYNTIA. Founded by the pioneers who shaped the scene, the Academy discovers, develops, and launches the next generation of world-class vocalists.

Under the visionary leadership of General Producer Masafumi "Marcy" Nishida — the iconic voice of EARTHSHAKER — raw talent is forged for the global stage.

♛
