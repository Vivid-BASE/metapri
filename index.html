ABOUT US

The era of Japanese girls' heavy metal has arrived. From the top of the US and European charts to sold-out world tours, Japanese female metal acts are a global phenomenon. As the world watches, a formidable new project rises from the heart of this movement.

Heavy Metal Princess Academy.

This is a premier training unit dedicated to forging the next generation of female vocalists, led by the original architects of the Japanese metal scene.

Under the visionary guidance of General Producer Masafumi "Marcy" Nishida (the legendary voice of EARTHSHAKER), we discover and nurture raw talent. Whether they are seasoned singers or newcomers who have never touched a microphone, we empower them to conquer the global stage.
